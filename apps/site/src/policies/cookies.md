Last updated 16 October 2026.

openaudit.se sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts, ads or trackers, so there is no cookie banner.

## What the site keeps in your browser

Two features keep a setting in your browser's local storage. Neither is sent to us, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes both.

| Key | Set when | Holds | Removed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | you switch between light and dark | `light` or `dark` | when you switch back to your system's theme |
| `openaudit-badge` | you register a [verified badge](/badge/) | the registration's id, repository, site address and verification code, so you can finish verifying later. Never the manage token | when you clear the site's data |

Both are strictly necessary for something you asked for, so under the ePrivacy rules they need no consent.

## Cloudflare

Cloudflare, which serves the site, can set a strictly necessary security cookie such as `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance` when it has to tell people from bots, for example after you pass a challenge. These cookies are Cloudflare's, build no profile of you, and are described in [Cloudflare's cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

## Page views

Page views are counted on the server, without cookies. The [privacy policy](/privacy/#visiting-the-site) says what is recorded.
