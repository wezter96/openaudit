# Code of Conduct

openaudit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People of every background, identity and experience level are welcome here.
- **Critique code, not people.** Keep reviews specific, constructive and about the work.
- **No harassment.** That includes insults, personal or political attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing other people's private information.
- **Report vulnerabilities responsibly.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and never use openaudit against systems you aren't authorised to test.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any community space where you represent the project, including [OpenSverige](https://opensverige.se/) channels when you're talking about openaudit.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through [@wezter96](https://github.com/wezter96) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban people who break this code, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
